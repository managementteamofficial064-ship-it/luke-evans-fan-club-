Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

How much does each membership cost?

The site is prepared for paid tiers, but the fees are intentionally not invented. Add your approved prices, currency, renewal terms, and refund policy before launch.

Does submitting the form issue my membership card?

No. The organiser must review the application, confirm the official fee and payment method, and approve the membership before issuing a card.

Is this an official celebrity website?

Not by default. This is an independent fan community template. Use the celebrity's name, photographs, trademarks, and official claims only when you have permission. Clearly disclose independent status where applicable.

How are requests sent?

The form opens the visitor's configured email app with a prepared message. The visitor must press Send. This starter site does not store applications online.
